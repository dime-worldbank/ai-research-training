The Big Number
~750
firms whose executives report AI productivity gains
larger than what revenue numbers show

A survey of nearly 750 corporate executives finds a productivity paradox. CFOs say AI is raising labor productivity by more than the gains implied by their own revenue and employment figures. The authors point to delayed output realization: the tools feel useful before the books catch up. Research teams see a version of this when they template a recurring deck or memo. The first pass often feels slower than copy-paste. Time savings show up on the second and third refill.

Source: Baslandze et al., NBER WP 34984 (2026)

In Case You Missed It

Using AI to Automate Research Outputs

Quick recap of the July 30 session (RG: September 16). There is a ready-made AI skill that turns manuscripts and results folders into DECDI-branded decks, with you approving the plan before any files change.

1.  Install the prepare-slides skill. It builds, improves, or rebrands research presentations in PowerPoint, Quarto/Reveal.js, or Beamer/LaTeX, using the bundled DECDI templates.
2.  Two phases: Plan and approve (outline, figure/table decisions, review checklist), then Build and verify (create or edit the deck, render, inspect). No files change until you approve the proposal.
3.  Exercise 1: create a new deck from a reproducibility package. Exercise 2: improve an existing PowerPoint using the paper and supporting outputs. Same skill, two starting points.
4.  The agent should not invent missing numbers, reconstruct unreported data, or create slides before you approve. If a figure only exists in the PDF, it proposes an adaptation or a placeholder.
5.  Plan the story first, then pick PowerPoint, Quarto, or Beamer when you build. Rebrand keeps slide order and wording. Improve can rewrite.

Materials: session page  ·  slides  ·  skill zip  ·  questions: dimeanalytics@worldbank.org

Try This Today  ·  10 minutes

Take the July 30 prepare-slides skill, drop it into one existing project, and run Phase 1 (Plan) only. Do not approve Build yet.

1. Download the skill: prepare-slides.zip.
2. At the project root: create .agents/skills/ and copy prepare-slides/ into it.
3. In your AI coding agent, type something like:

Read prepare-slides/SKILL.md and propose a 20-minute policy deck from [manuscript / results folder]. Audience: [counterparts]. Do not create files yet.

Read the outline and figure/table decision table. When it asks to build, say not yet. The plan is the 10-minute exercise.

Have more than 10 minutes? Practice on the session demo projects (create from a package, or improve a deck). Or turn one recurring results memo into a refillable Quarto/LaTeX shell. See this issue’s Deep Dive.
