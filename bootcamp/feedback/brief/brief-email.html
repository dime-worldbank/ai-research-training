AI-Enabled Research Bootcamp
Results brief · October 1–8, 2026 · Led by Impact Analytics (Development Impact) with the Development Data Group and ITS
98 staff. 5 days. Agentic AI tools installed on every participant's computer. In the first-ever AI-Enabled Research Bootcamp, participants learned by doing, working with coding agents, memory files, and reusable skills across the research cycle, with instructors in the room throughout to provide customized support. Participant feedback was overwhelmingly positive. Here is where participants started, where they got to, and what comes next.

What participants gained
After the week, 83% felt confident directing an AI agent, and participants expect to use about 70% of the content.

Next steps
  • Turning intent into practice. One output of the Bootcamp was a practical AI adoption plan, and nearly all participants (97.9%) plan to apply what they learned within six weeks.
  • Sustained adoption. Regular Office Hours will provide customized support for sustained adoption of AI tools and skills, and a follow-up survey in two months will measure it.
  • Building on demand. Interest reaches beyond DEC (15 VPUs), and some participants are already recommending the Bootcamp. We are brainstorming approaches to scaling training with colleagues in DECDG and ITS.
  • Open materials. Course materials are live and public: dime-worldbank.github.io/ai-research-training/bootcamp.
