Module 1: Current Use of Generative AI

  • 98% of respondents (63 answered) currently use generative AI for their job.
  • Most-used tasks: Documents (85%), Background Research (82%), Email (69%).
  • 65% have had AI-assisted work included in a report, paper, or other output.
  • Of those, only 28% gave a clear disclosure statement — most either omitted details or wanted to disclose but weren't sure how.

Module 2: AI Workflows, Impact & Verification

  • Half of respondents (50%) don't know what an AI memory file is, and a similar share (37%) don't know what a reusable AI skill is.
  • Produce higher-quality outputs (66%) is the most widely reported effect of AI on analytical work.
  • Nearly half (45%) see no change in reproducibility from using AI, and only 18% report it's improved.
  • Manually review all outputs (77%) is the most common way respondents verify AI-generated output.

Module 3: GitHub, Copilot & Safe AI Use

  • 38% of respondents are not familiar with GitHub at all.
  • Only 23% have a WBG GitHub Copilot license (active or exploring); most have either never used it or never heard of it.
  • On the safe-use knowledge check, responses split almost evenly between two approaches (41% and 41%), with 17% unsure — no one chose the two most restrictive options.

Module 4: Background & Expectations

  • Tenure is concentrated in the middle: 33% have 5–9 years at the WBG and another 33% have 10–19 years.
  • Top expectations for the Bootcamp: Apply new tools to my job (94%), Increase my productivity (86%), Improve quality of my work (79%).
  • 75% have not taken an AI-for-research course before.
  • 40% rate their current AI-for-research knowledge as poor or very poor.
