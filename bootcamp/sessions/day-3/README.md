# Day 3 (October 7): AI Tools for Reproducible Research Products

## Description

### Objective
Participants learn how to use AI across the full research production pipeline — from synthesizing literature and drafting reports, to creating dynamic presentations, to building reproducibility packages that allow others to verify and build on their findings. By the end of the day, staff will have hands-on experience with reusable AI workflows for producing polished, publication-ready research outputs, and will understand how to publish data in AI-ready formats that maximize the impact and reusability of their work. At the end of the day, participants will reflect on how they will use AI going forward, what skills and workflows are most applicable for their project, and anticipated constraints. They will create specific adoption targets for their project, which we will monitor through a follow-up survey.

### Expected Outcomes
- Participants use AI skills to create a presentation or policy brief for their own project
- Participants create and verify a reproducibility package for their own project
- Participants create an AI adoption plan, specifying how they will use AI going forward, with specific 6-week and 3-month targets
