# Referee report (fictional — demo material)

Received 2024 on the author's previous paper ("Textbook Provision and Test
Scores in Eastern Province", fictional). Included as Protocol C material.

## Summary
The paper studies a relevant question with unusually good administrative
data. I have two substantive concerns and one presentational one.

## Major comments

1. **Identification.** The rollout of textbook provision was determined by
   the provincial office and prioritised low-performing schools. The paper
   asserts the rollout is "plausibly exogenous" but presents no pre-trend
   evidence and no discussion of how prioritisation would bias the estimates.
   Absent an event-study figure or a credible argument, the causal language
   should be substantially weakened.

2. **Overclaiming.** The conclusion recommends national adoption and asserts
   cost-effectiveness, but the manuscript generalises far beyond its setting
   and contains no cost data. The results concern one province and two
   cohorts; the recommendations should match.

## Minor comment

3. Table captions are not self-contained (clustering and sample not stated);
   several numbers in the text differ from the tables at the second decimal.
