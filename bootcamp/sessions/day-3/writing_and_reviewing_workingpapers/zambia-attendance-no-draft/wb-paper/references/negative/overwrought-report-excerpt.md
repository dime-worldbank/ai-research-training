It is widely acknowledged that, in the context of the multifaceted and
evolving landscape of human capital development, the question of educational
attainment outcomes represents a crucial and increasingly salient policy
domain. Against this backdrop, and taking into consideration the various
structural, institutional, and socio-economic factors at play, this report
seeks to delve into the complex interplay of determinants that may
potentially be associated, in certain contexts and under certain conditions,
with observed patterns of school participation. It should be noted at the
outset that the findings presented herein should be interpreted with
considerable caution, given the inherent limitations of the available data
infrastructure and the multiplicity of confounding factors that may
potentially influence the outcomes of interest. Furthermore, a comprehensive
approach necessitates the holistic consideration of a wide spectrum of
stakeholder perspectives across the education ecosystem, with a view to
paving the way for evidence-informed policy dialogue going forward.
