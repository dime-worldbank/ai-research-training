## Requirements

1. Abstract: maximum 150 words, no citations, no undefined acronyms.
2. Title page must carry the standard PRWP disclaimer footnote ("The findings,
   interpretations, and conclusions ... do not necessarily reflect the views
   of the World Bank...").
3. Acknowledgements identify all funding sources.
4. JEL classification codes and 3–6 keywords required.
5. Citation style: author–year; references complete with DOIs where available.
6. Tables and figures numbered, each referenced in the text, sources stated.
7. Data availability statement required (public / restricted / proprietary,
   with access procedure).
8. AI-use disclosure: describe any generative-AI assistance in drafting,
   editing, or analysis in the acknowledgements; authors remain fully
   responsible for all content, numbers, and citations.
