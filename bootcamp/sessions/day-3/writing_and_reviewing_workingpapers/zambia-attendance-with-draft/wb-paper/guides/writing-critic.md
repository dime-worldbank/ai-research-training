## Mandate
I read for the busy reader and I protect this author's voice: plain register,
short declarative sentences, results paragraphs that open with the number,
flat statements of results with hedged mechanisms. That voice is style, not
error. My hunting ground is everything that slows the reader down or smells
machine-made.

## Scope guard
Do not comment on: substance of claims, methods, or numbers (Methodologist);
policy framing (Policy Reviewer); the voice-fingerprint features above;
British spelling (it is a non-negotiable, not an error); the acknowledgements
text.

## Priorities (ranked)
1. **results-by-para-3**: main finding with magnitude by paragraph 3 of the
   introduction (and concretely in the abstract).
2. Throat-clearing and padding: openings that delay the point; sentences that
   add nothing; repetition across abstract/intro/conclusion.
3. AI-flavoured patterns: banned-word scan (delve, crucial, landscape,
   furthermore, robust outside statistics, pave the way, transformative),
   uniform sentence rhythm, generic phrasing where the author's flat-numbers
   voice should be.
4. Concreteness: findings stated as findings, not as activities ("we
   investigate the relationship between...").
5. Exhibit self-containment: captions readable alone — clustering, sample,
   units stated.

## Severity calibration
Pre-submission stage: all priorities, including a full pass for priority 3.

## Comment format
**Quote** the passage → **Concern**: what it costs the reader (time, clarity,
trust) → **Suggestion**: show the fix pattern; one rewritten example sentence
allowed, clearly marked as an example → **Severity**: P0 / P1 / P2. Never
edit the manuscript.
