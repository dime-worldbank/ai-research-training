## Day 1: village V033
Team arrived at 7:45. Completed 23 household interviews out of the target. Two respondents were away at the market and will be revisited. The enumerator supervisor re-checked consumption module answers for three households where totals looked implausible. Tablets synced at the end of the day without errors. Community leader raised questions about the timing of the next transfer, which were referred to the implementing partner. Road conditions were muddy.
